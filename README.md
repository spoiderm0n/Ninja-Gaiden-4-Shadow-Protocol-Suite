## 📜 Overview

There is a certain kind of player who does not merely *play* a game — they *curate* it.

**Ninja Gaiden 4 Save Vault & Progression Companion** is built for exactly that player. Rather than treating your save files as disposable checkpoints, this companion treats them as a personal archive: a living record of every stance mastered, every essence collected, and every legendary encounter survived. Think of it as a librarian for your ninja's legacy — quiet, precise, and always watching the shelves.

This repository is the reference home for the **2026 Edition** release line. It documents the companion's capabilities, the reasoning behind its architecture, the languages it speaks, and the philosophy that guides its ongoing development.

If you enjoy games where reflexes and patience define mastery, this companion simply extends that same discipline to the way you organize your achievements.

---

## 🎯 What This Project Actually Does

At its core, the companion is a **local-first progression assistant**. It reads the standard save structure produced by Ninja Gaiden 4 on Windows 10 and Windows 11, presents that information in a clean and responsive interface, and gives you tools to understand your run in richer detail.

It does **not** alter the game's checksum, does **not** touch online services, and does **not** interact with other players. It is a single-player preservation utility — a magnifying glass, not a skeleton key.

## 🧠 Design Philosophy

Most companion tools treat the player as someone who wants shortcuts. This project takes the opposite stance.

The philosophy here is **enrichment over alteration**. Every feature exists to help you *understand* your run, never to bypass it. A ninja who has genuinely earned their rank knows the difference — and so does the community that gathers around this repository.

We think of the companion as a *dojo ledger*: it records your training, your battles, and your evolution, but it never fights for you.

## 🤝 Community & Support Desk

A tool like this lives or dies by its users. The support desk operates on a rotating volunteer schedule so that someone is nearly always awake and answering.

When you ask a question, you are asked to include:

1. Your Windows build number.
2. The companion version you are running.
3. A short description of what you expected versus what occurred.
4. Any relevant screenshots, cropped to avoid exposing personal information.

That last point matters. Even in a community built on trust, discretion is a virtue.

---

## 🔐 Privacy Stance

No telemetry. No analytics beacons. No silent connections. The companion writes to your disk and reads from your disk — nothing else. If a future feature ever required network access, it would be strictly opt-in and loudly documented.

This is not a marketing promise. It is an architectural commitment.
